THE COMMANDER’S COMPANION

      
Your field guide

      

        

          
Monarch

          

            Choose one of ten fantasy peoples before joining. Stories and
            bonuses appear under People & Civilization. Barracks train early
            infantry, ranges train each people’s bow units, and stables open in
            Feudal Age. Highlanders and Orcs throw spears instead of building
            ranges. Dwarves use crossbows; Orcs, Goblins and Dwarves ride hogs.
          

          

            Castle Age unlocks the 3 × 3 Fortress and your people’s unique
            champion. Warlords and Duet Bards grant nearby allied units 12%
            movement and 10% attack, without stacking. Ranged garrisons add one
            shot each to defensive buildings, up to seven.
          

        

        

          
Repairs & research

          

            Select villagers and use the Commands, Economy buildings or Military
            buildings categories. Right-click a damaged friendly building, or
            press R and click it, to repair. Repairs consume half the original
            construction resources per full health bar restored.
          

          

            Build a School for gathering and villager walking upgrades. In the
            Feudal Age, build a Blacksmith for soldier attack and armor
            upgrades. Select the structure to research; its price and age
            requirements appear on hover.
          

        

        

          
01   An economy first

          

            Select villagers. Right-click berry bushes for food, trees for wood,
            and deposits for gold or stone. Workers carry resources back to the
            Town Center. Build farms for lasting food.
          

        

        

          
02   A place to call home

          

            With villagers selected, choose a building below, then click visible
            empty ground. The workers walk over and construct it. Houses
            increase the population limit. Right-click cancels placement; Escape
            opens the game menu.
          

        

        

          
03   Raise your banner

          

            Select your Town Center to train villagers. Barracks train spearmen.
            Advance to the Feudal Age for archers and towers, then the Castle
            Age for knights. Spearmen counter cavalry.
          

        

        

          
04   Across the river

          

            Use either bridge to reach the rival kingdom. Right-click enemies to
            attack. Use Attack or Attack-move, then click a target or
            destination. Patrol travels back and forth; Defend returns to its
            area. Destroy their final Town Center to win. AI kingdoms build and
            raid in solo or LAN games. Matching team numbers share vision and
            victory.
          

        

        

          
05   Rally and shelter

          

            Select a production building and right-click to set its rally flag.
            A resource flag sends new villagers to gather. Right-click your Town
            Center or tower with units selected to shelter them; select the
            building and Deploy garrison to release them. Town Centers can also
            train villagers directly inside.
          

        

        

          
06   Your next orders

          

            Shift + right-click queues movement flags. Units follow them in
            order, finding routes around obstacles. Escape opens audio,
            controls, info and surrender. Confirming Surrender returns to the
            main menu. Defeated LAN commanders watch only their team's shared
            vision, with fog of war.
          

        

      

      

        Delete removes your selected units immediately. Buildings require two
        confirmation clicks before razing. Fallen units keep their last sight
        for five game seconds before fog returns; surviving units and buildings
        keep revealing normally. Damaged buildings progress through smoke,
        embers, fire and structural damage.

Map properties

            
            

              
            

            
            

              Starts reserve a dry 17 × 17 area for a fair settlement. Paint
              bridges to connect land. The inner 3 × 3 outline is the Town
              Center; resource painting keeps it clear.
            

            
            

          

        

        

          Left-drag paints · middle-drag pans · wheel zooms · undo restores a
            stroke
        

      

      

        

          

            
Start with data, not code

            

              Create or duplicate a civilization. Set its name, story, starting
              stockpile and multipliers. Save it, then choose it in the welcome
              screen or your lobby row. The operator stores data in
              data/content.json.
            

          

          

            
Design a battlefield

            

              Choose dimensions and 2–16 starts. Paint land, water, bridges,
              woodland and deposits. Check that all starts have land routes,
              then save. The host can choose your map in the lobby.
            

          

          

            
Share a mod

            

              Export JSON and send the file to the server operator. They import
              it and save it on their server. Imported files are validated; they
              do not execute scripts. A player can export a draft even without
              permission to save on a live server.
            

          

          

            
Go beyond the editors

            

              games/crownfall/catalog.h defines the roster and
              costs. games/crownfall/models.h defines moddable
              data. games/crownfall/world.cpp contains the rules.
              web/art.js generates the single sprite image. Follow
              docs/MODDING.md to add mechanics and artwork.
            

          

        

        

          Saving a mod does not change a match already in progress. Restart or
          create a new match to apply it. Save and export important designs
          before removing local data.
