# Exact tile placement

Building footprints are square grid cells: Town Center 3 × 3 (nine); House, Farm, Barracks, Archery Range, Stable, Blacksmith, School and Watchtower 2 × 2 (four); Wall 1 × 1. Every natural resource is one cell, centered at `(tileX + 0.5, tileY + 0.5)`. Multiple resources cannot occupy the same cell or spawn inside a building. Generation reserves every kingdom’s Town Center and nonoverlapping house first, places its crew on free exits, then scatters resources. Starting units also reserve their cells against resource spawning. This order matters on small, crowded maps.

The engine snaps the clicked tile to the center of the footprint. Odd-sized footprints have half-coordinate centers; even-sized footprints have whole-coordinate centers between their four central tiles. All occupied cells must be dry, visible, in bounds and free of another building or resource. Adjacent structures may touch without sharing cells. Trees and natural deposits also block their one occupied cell. Farms reserve four construction cells while remaining walkable for workers.

The same definitions drive occupancy, placement previews, click selection, interaction ranges, unit exits and editor Town Center outlines. Resource bases remain inside their projected tile diamonds, while trees retain tall foliage and visible trunks. Buildings have full-resolution upright art with ground anchors: their foundations remain inside the diamond while roofs and towers rise above it. Units also keep a one-tile ground footprint with upright poses and continuous movement. Construction, damage and collapse share these bounds. Visible roof/canopy pixels can be selected without enlarging occupied tiles; transparent space between objects still clicks through. Villagers can gather by right-clicking either a resource's visible artwork or its occupied tile.

Turn on the grid with **G** to inspect alignment. In the map editor each start marks the 3 × 3 Town Center; painted resources occupy a unique cell. Older resource coordinates are normalized when loaded, and collisions are skipped when the world is generated. Stable resource IDs remain 11–14.

Modders: edit `Definition.footprint` in `games/crownfall/catalog.h`, keep `CrownArt.footprint` and generated art bounds synchronized, and rebuild every export runtime after changing simulation rules. One size is a side length, not an area: size 2 reserves four cells.
